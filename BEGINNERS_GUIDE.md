# Beginner's Guide - Moscow Railways Train Simulator

## Welcome, Train Driver!

This guide will help you understand how to play the game and what to do before departure time.

## First Time Playing?

### Step 1: Starting the Game
1. Open `index.html` in your web browser
2. Click **"New Game"** on the main menu
3. You'll be taken to the **Depot** where you select your train

### Step 2: Choose Your Train
1. You start with **ЭР22** (Legendary Soviet train) and **ЭМ4** (Modern electric)
2. Click on a train to select it
3. Each train has different:
   - **Max Speed** - how fast it can go
   - **Acceleration** - how quickly it speeds up
   - **Capacity** - how many passengers it can carry

**Tip**: Start with **ЭР22** to learn the basics - it's reliable and forgiving!

### Step 3: Choose Your Route
1. You start with **МЦД-1** (Одинцово → Лобня)
2. This route has **27 stations** over **65 km**
3. Click on the route to start

### Step 4: Before Departure - IMPORTANT!

You start at **Одинцово station** at **6:00 AM**.
Your first departure is at **6:30 AM**.

**What to do in these 30 minutes:**

1. **Learn the Controls** (Practice them!)
   - **↑ Arrow** - Accelerate
   - **↓ Arrow** - Brake
   - **SPACE** - Emergency Brake (quick stop)
   - **D** - Open/Close Doors
   - **H** - Horn (for fun and realism)
   - **L** - Toggle Lights
   - **C** - Cruise Control (maintain speed)
   - **TAB** - View full timetable
   - **ESC** - Pause

2. **Check Your Timetable** (Press TAB)
   - Your first stop: **Баковка at 6:33 AM** (2.4 km away)
   - Second stop: **Сколково at 6:36 AM** (4.6 km away)
   - Last stop: **Лобня at 8:12 AM** (65.2 km away)

3. **Understand the HUD** (look at your screen)
   - **Top Left**: Speed and EXP
   - **Top Right**: Current time
   - **Right Side**: Upcoming stations with distances
   - **Bottom**: Controls and train status

4. **Wait and Relax**
   - Game time runs at **12x speed** (5 minutes real = 1 hour game time)
   - You can use this time to familiarize yourself with the interface
   - At 6:30 AM, you're ready to depart!

## During Your First Trip

### Starting Your Journey (6:30 AM)

1. **Press ↑** to start accelerating
2. Watch your speed increase
3. **Target Speed**: 60-80 km/h between stations
4. **Distance to Баковка**: 2.4 km

### Approaching a Station

1. **2 km before**: Start slowing down
2. **0.5 km before**: Brake harder
3. **Stop when**: Speed < 5 km/h AND within 50m of station
4. **Check distance**: Shows in "Upcoming Stations" panel

### At the Station

1. **Press D** to open doors (only works when stopped!)
2. **Watch**: Passengers will board/exit (green notifications!)
3. **Wait**: 10-15 seconds for passenger exchange
4. **Press W**: Warning sound "Doors closing!"
5. **Press D**: Close doors
6. **Press ↑**: Accelerate to next station

### Pro Tips for Stations

- **Yellow line on platform**: This is the safety line
- **Green notifications**: "+X EXP (Passengers delivered)" means you're earning!
- **Red notifications**: Penalties (late, skipped station, etc.)
- **Platform length**: Matches your train exactly

### Using Cruise Control

1. **Accelerate to desired speed** (e.g., 80 km/h)
2. **Press C** to enable cruise control
3. **Train maintains speed automatically**
4. **Press C again** to disable
5. **Brake or accelerate** to override

**When to use**: On long stretches between stations!

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

### ❌ Don't:
1. **Skip stations** (-50 EXP penalty!)
2. **Be more than 2 minutes late/early** (-1 EXP per minute)
3. **Stop with train hanging off platform** (-10 EXP per unsafe door)
4. **Forget to open doors** (no passengers = no EXP!)
5. **Go too fast approaching stations** (you'll overshoot!)

### ✅ Do:
1. **Start braking early** (1-2 km before station)
2. **Open doors at every station** (+1 EXP per passenger!)
3. **Check timetable often** (TAB key)
4. **Use cruise control** on long stretches
5. **Save progress** (pause menu → Save and Exit)

## Understanding EXP (Experience Points)

### How to Earn EXP:
- **+1 EXP** per passenger delivered to their destination
- **+100 EXP** for completing the route (minus lateness penalty)
- **Typical route**: 300-500 EXP if done well

### What EXP Unlocks:
- **100 EXP**: ЭД4М train
- **400 EXP**: ЭШ2 (Wide-body electric)
- **500 EXP**: ЭС2Г "Ласточка" (High-speed)
- **800 EXP**: ЭП2Д (Double-decker express)
- **300 EXP**: МЦД-2 route

### How to Lose EXP:
- **-50 EXP**: Skipping a station
- **-10 EXP**: Unsafe door positioning
- **-1 EXP/min**: Being late or early (> 2 minutes)

## Sample First Run Strategy

### Одинцово to Баковка (First Station)

**Time**: 6:30 AM → 6:33 AM (3 minutes)
**Distance**: 2.4 km
**Strategy**:
1. **6:30 AM**: Accelerate to 60 km/h
2. **6:31 AM**: At ~1.5 km, maintain speed
3. **6:32 AM**: At ~0.5 km, start braking
4. **6:33 AM**: Stop at Баковка, open doors
5. **6:33:30 AM**: Close doors, depart for Сколково

### Баковка to Сколково (Second Station)

**Time**: 6:33 AM → 6:36 AM (3 minutes)
**Distance**: 2.2 km (from Баковка)
**Strategy**:
1. Same pattern as before
2. Accelerate, cruise, brake, stop
3. This is your rhythm for the whole route!

## Advanced Tips

### Speed Management
- **City sections** (Одинцово to Кунцевская): 60-80 km/h
- **Suburban sections** (after Савёловская): 80-100 km/h
- **Final approach**: Always < 10 km/h

### Time Management
- **Check clock vs scheduled time** constantly
- **If early**: Slow down between stations
- **If late**: Speed up (but be careful at stops!)

### Passenger Management
- **More passengers** board at busy stations (morning rush)
- **They exit** at random future stations
- **Maximum efficiency**: Stop at EVERY station

## Troubleshooting

### "I missed a station!"
- **Result**: -50 EXP penalty
- **Solution**: Be more careful with next one
- **Prevention**: Start braking at 2 km before station

### "I'm always late!"
- **Cause**: Too slow between stations
- **Solution**: Maintain 70-80 km/h cruising speed
- **Tip**: Use cruise control!

### "I can't stop in time!"
- **Cause**: Braking too late or going too fast
- **Solution**: Start braking at 1.5-2 km before station
- **Tip**: Emergency brake (SPACE) for urgent stops

### "My train won't accelerate!"
- **Check**: Are doors open? (Close them with D)
- **Check**: Is emergency brake engaged?
